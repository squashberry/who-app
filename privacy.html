WHO · PRIVACY DIRECTION

Your phonebook is not ours.

WHO is in beta. This page describes the privacy architecture planned for future cloud-backed features. Contact intelligence will be opt-in; app backups are intended to live under the user's own Google account; admin credentials will remain server-side.

Contact intelligence

WHO may let users contribute selected contact information to improve identity suggestions. The product should request permission, clearly explain the purpose, and let users opt out.

Google Drive backups

WHO plans to use the user's Google account for private backups. Google Drive's application-data area can store app-specific files that are hidden from normal Drive browsing and are only accessible to the app.

Admin and telemetry

Public app clients should never carry admin secrets. Operational data should be minimized, access-controlled, and retained only as long as the product needs it.
