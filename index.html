THE NEXT UPDATE

Names become useful when people agree.

The next WHO intelligence layer is planned around consented contact contributions. Multiple devices can contribute a name for the same normalized number; the service can then rank candidates instead of trusting one upload blindly.

01
NormalizeConvert phone numbers into one consistent format.
02
CompareAggregate opt-in name contributions from participating users.
03
ConfidenceShow a candidate and confidence indicator rather than pretending certainty.
PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your contacts are yours.

WHO’s future contact-intelligence features are designed to be opt-in. Backups are intended to live under the user’s own Google account. Admin systems are separated from public product data.

✓ Contribution can be disabled
✓ Backup belongs to your Google account
✓ Admin secrets stay server-side
✓ Service outages should not brick the app
