THE NEXT UPDATE

Names become useful when people agree.

The next WHO intelligence layer is planned around consented contact contributions. Multiple devices can contribute a name for the same normalized number; the service can then rank candidates instead of trusting one upload blindly.

01
NormalizeConvert phone numbers into one consistent format.
02
CompareAggregate opt-in name contributions from participating users.
03
ConfidenceShow a candidate and confidence indicator rather than pretending certainty.
PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your contacts are yours.

WHO’s future contact-intelligence features are designed to be opt-in. Backups are intended to live under the user’s own Google account. Admin systems are separated from public product data.

✓ Contribution can be disabled
✓ Backup belongs to your Google account
✓ Admin secrets stay server-side
✓ Service outages should not brick the app

HOW WHO THINKS

From an unknown number
to useful context.

WHO is not trying to guess everything instantly. The product is being built around a simple chain: collect permitted signals, normalize them, compare them, explain the result and let the user decide what to do.

01
⌕

Detect

When a call arrives, WHO can work with the phone number, the local contacts database and available caller signals.

INPUT · CALL + CONTACTS
02
◉

Recognize

Known contact information can be combined with anonymized caller observations and other community signals.

COMPARE · MULTIPLE SIGNALS
03
✓

Explain

WHO is designed to show the confidence and source of a useful caller signal instead of pretending every match is certain.

OUTPUT · CONTEXT + CONFIDENCE

Act

Answer, ignore, block or report. The caller screen stays focused on what you need in the moment.

COMMUNITY SIGNALS

When something looks wrong,
give WHO a signal.

WHO already has caller lookup, observation and reporting paths in its backend architecture. The public product is designed so that reporting can feed the caller-intelligence layer without turning one person's opinion into unquestioned truth.

01
ReportFlag a number when the call feels unwanted or suspicious.
02
AggregateCombine multiple observations instead of trusting a single report.
03
ImproveUse stronger community evidence to improve future caller context.
